# Demo media

Every file here was drawn by `scripts/demo_media.py`, from shapes and text alone. There are no photographs, no
real people and nothing taken from anyone else's work. Each picture says "DEMO · generated illustration" in a
corner, and the profile cards and avatar say DEMO too.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate these files to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`hunt demo` (`hunt/demo.py`) builds the fictional demo case from them. Dorian Vell, his accounts, posts,
employer and records are invented.
