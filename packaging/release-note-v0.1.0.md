**Hunt 0.1.0** is the first release of a free, open-source, local-first investigation tool for background checks, insurance claims, due diligence and social media reports. It runs on your Mac (Apple Silicon), and your cases, evidence and the AI that reads photos and videos never leave it.
- **Finding:** start from a name, username, email, phone number, address or profile link. Hunt checks over a thousand sites and a set of public records, shows which accounts really belong to the person and why, and leaves the decision to you.
- **Looking:** it reads public posts where it can do so logged out. On Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and LinkedIn, it records only what you view yourself, signed in as you, in the Hunt Browser. A media scan, on your Mac, flags activity such as lifting after an injury date.
- **Reporting:** every sentence in the report cites its evidence. A fingerprinted evidence bundle comes with a description of how the evidence was collected, for FRE 902(13) certification.
- **Over a claim:** Check again and Watch this subject follow the subject's accounts for as long as the claim runs, and cases move between Macs in one encrypted file.

To install, see the [user guide](https://github.com/als7294/hunt/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md); [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/als7294/hunt/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) lists what's in this release and what isn't built yet. Hunt is for lawful, purpose-bound investigations: [Hunt's rules](https://github.com/als7294/hunt/blob/main/docs/research/legal.md#hunts-rules). It uses free and open sources only, and complements licensed data tools rather than replacing them. AGPL-3.0.
