# Practitioner interview

The ranked pain points in [`pain-points.md`](pain-points.md) come from public sources. These questions check them against a working investigator's actual day. About 20 minutes; answers can be short.

## Your workflow today
1. Walk through your last social media report from intake to delivery. Which steps took the longest?
2. What does the "central dashboard" you use look like, and which tools do you actually open from it most?
3. What information do you usually start with? (name only, name + DOB, email, handle, address…)
4. Roughly how many cases a week, and how long does a typical one take?

## Accuracy
5. How often do you land on the wrong person with the same or a similar name? How do you rule them out today?
6. What's the minimum you need before you'll write "this account belongs to the subject"?

## Evidence and reports
7. How do you capture evidence now (screenshots, PDF prints, Hunchly…)? Has a capture ever been challenged?
8. What does your report template look like? Which sections are hardest to write?
9. Is any of your work employment screening under FCRA? Does your firm require anything specific (consent, redactions)?

## Photos and location
10. How often do you need to work out where a photo was taken? What do you do today?

## Tools and limits
11. Which tools have broken or disappeared on you in the last year? Which paid tools would you never give up, and why?
12. What would make you trust a new tool's results, and what would make you stop using it immediately?
